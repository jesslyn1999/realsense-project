Capture guide

Best ChArUco scanning workflow

  1. Make frame 1 the best view. Keep the board nearly front-facing, sharply focused, and visible beside the object. Aim for at least 30 valid depth corners and reprojection RMSE below 0.5 px.
  2. Move in small steps. Translate only 2–5 cm or rotate 5–10° between captures.
  3. Preserve surface overlap. Keep at least 60% of the same object surface visible between neighboring captures. Add intermediate captures before moving to another side.
  4. Keep the scene fixed. The object and ChArUco board must not move during the session.
  5. Let the camera settle. Pause after moving so exposure and depth stabilize, then capture without camera motion.
  6. Use depth-friendly lighting. Prefer diffuse light and avoid glare, deep shadows, reflective surfaces, and board occlusion.
  7. Keep a practical distance. Stay roughly 35–60 cm from the board and avoid steep viewing angles.

Each new frame needs a strong registration connection to at least one earlier frame; it does not need to overlap every frame.
